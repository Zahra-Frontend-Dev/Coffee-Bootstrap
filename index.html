why Bloom cafa?

We are committed to providing quality products and services and always strive to meet your needs in the best way.

For many people in Western societies, coffee is one of the largest sources of antioxidants in the diet (even more than many fruits and vegetables). These antioxidants fight free radicals and prevent cell damage. Drinking coffee has been linked to a reduced risk of depression and suicidal desire in some studies. Caffeine helps improve mood by stimulating the production of "good" chemicals in the brain, such as dopamine and serotonin.

Coffee grows in the tropics, between geographical orbits called the "coffee belt". Famous countries in coffee production include Brazil, Colombia, Ethiopia, Vietnam and Costa Rica. The coffee tree takes about 3 to 4 years to bear fruit and produce a fruit called "coffee cherry". Inside these cherries are coffee beans.

(Washed Process): In this method, the beans are separated from the coffee cherries and immersed in water. Then, by drying, the remaining layer is separated from the grain. This method creates a clear and balanced taste in coffee (Natural Process): In this method, the cherries are completely dried along with the skin and flesh and then separated. This process gives the seeds a rich and fruity taste..
